LOPEZ MENU - PUT THE WEBSITE ONLINE (only clicking, no Terminal)
=================================================================

You need: the file  lopez-menu-upload.zip  and a free Cloudflare account.
Time: about 10 minutes, once. Button names may differ slightly; the words are close.

Do not unzip the file. Do not use the old  lopez-site.zip  for uploading.


PART 1 - Put the site online
----------------------------
1. Go to https://dash.cloudflare.com and log in.
2. In the menu on the left, click  "Workers & Pages"
   (it can be inside  "Compute (Workers)"  - click that first if you see it).

   IF YOU ALREADY HAVE A PROJECT (for example "lopez-menu") from your earlier try:
   3. Click your project's name.
   4. Click the  "Create deployment"  button (top right).
   5. Drag  lopez-menu-upload.zip  into the upload box (or click "select from computer").
   6. Click  "Save and Deploy".  Wait until it says "Success". Go to PART 2.

   IF YOU HAVE NO PROJECT YET:
   3. Click the blue  "Create"  button (or "Create application").
   4. Click the  "Pages"  tab. (If you only see Workers, click the link
      "Looking to deploy Pages? Get started" at the bottom.)
   5. Next to "Drag and drop your files" / "Upload assets", click  "Get started"  / "Upload assets".
   6. Project name: type  lopez-menu   -> click  "Create project".
   7. Drag  lopez-menu-upload.zip  into the upload box -> click  "Deploy site".
   8. Wait for "Success", then click  "Continue to project".

   The menu now works at  https://lopez-menu.pages.dev
   (hearts, messages and the owner's page start working after PART 2-4).


PART 2 - Create the database (stores likes, messages and your menu changes)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. In the menu on the left, click  "Storage & Databases"  ->  "D1 SQL Database".
2. Click  "Create"  (or "Create database").
3. Name: type  lopez-db   -> click  "Create".
   Nothing else to do here - the site sets the database up by itself.


PART 3 - Connect the database and set your password
---------------------------------------------------
1. Left menu -> "Workers & Pages" -> click  lopez-menu.
2. Click the  "Settings"  tab.
3. Find  "Bindings"  -> click  "+ Add"  ->  choose  "D1 database".
      Variable name:   DB          (exactly these two capital letters)
      D1 database:     lopez-db    (pick it from the list)
   Click  "Save".
4. Still in Settings, find  "Variables and Secrets"  -> click  "+ Add".
      Type:            Secret
      Variable name:   ADMIN_PASSWORD      (exactly like this)
      Value:           your owner password (at least 12 characters; write it down)
   Click  "Save".
   If Cloudflare asks "Production" or "Preview", choose  Production.


PART 4 - Upload once more (needed so the settings from PART 3 take effect)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Click the  "Deployments"  tab of lopez-menu.
2. Click  "Create deployment"  -> drag  lopez-menu-upload.zip  ->  "Save and Deploy".


PART 5 - Check that everything works
------------------------------------
1. Open  https://lopez-menu.pages.dev  on your phone.
2. Tap a heart: after a second a number "1" appears next to it.
3. At the bottom, send a test message in "Écrivez-nous".
4. Open  https://lopez-menu.pages.dev/admin/  and sign in with your password.
   You should see the test message. Delete it.

If something is wrong, the owner's page tells you:
  "Base D1 non configurée / D1 binding DB missing"   -> PART 3 step 3 is missing or misspelled,
                                                        or PART 4 was not done.
  "ADMIN_PASSWORD non défini"                         -> PART 3 step 4 is missing, or PART 4 not done.
  "Mot de passe incorrect"                            -> you typed a different password.
  Hearts show no numbers / "L'envoi a échoué"         -> same as the first line.


USING THE OWNER'S PAGE  (https://lopez-menu.pages.dev/admin/)
------------------------------------------------------------
  Messages  - customer messages; mark as read or delete.
  Menu      - tap "Modifier / Edit" on any item: change name, description (French and
              English), price, photo, or untick "Visible" to hide it. Tap "Enregistrer / Save".
              "+ Ajouter / Add" adds a new item to a category.
              Changes appear on the menu within seconds. No need to upload anything again.
  J'aime    - every item ranked by number of likes.

Only upload a new ZIP when Claude sends you one. Your changes made on the owner's page
are kept in the database and are not lost when you upload a new ZIP.

Good to know
  - Everything here is on Cloudflare's free plan.
  - Each visitor can send 5 messages per 10 minutes and like/unlike 60 times per 10 minutes.
  - 10 wrong passwords lock sign-in for 15 minutes.
  - You get no phone notification for new messages; open the owner's page to check.
  - To change the password: PART 3 step 4 (edit ADMIN_PASSWORD), then PART 4.


For developers: the source is in lopez-site/ (public/ = site, functions/ + lib/ = server).
tools/build.py rebuilds public/menu-data.json; tools/make_upload.py builds lopez-menu-upload.zip
(compiles functions/ into _worker.js, which dashboard uploads need).
